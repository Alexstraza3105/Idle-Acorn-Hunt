Privacy notice

Acorn Hunt · Last updated September 24, 2026

What we collect

If you create an account, we store:

  • your email address and password (handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase; we never see your password in readable form)
  • your username and chosen squirrel
  • your game progress, so it can carry over between devices

If you play without an account, your progress stays only in your own browser on your device.

How it's used

Only to run the game: signing you in and saving and loading your progress. We don't sell your data, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers at the moment.

Other services involved

  • Supabase stores account and save data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the game and may log visitors' IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr supply a font and a code library, so your browser contacts them when the game loads.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time, and your progress stays on your device.
  • To have your account and saved data deleted, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If we add things like ads or payments, we'll update this page first.
